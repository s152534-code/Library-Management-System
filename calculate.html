How the total is calculated

Overdue fine = books × days overdue × OMR 0.200, up to OMR 5.000 per book.

Active students receive a 25% discount on the overdue fine. Printing costs OMR 0.025 per page.

Total = overdue fine − discount + printing cost.

These are sample project rates, not official university library charges.
