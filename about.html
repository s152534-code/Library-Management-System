About Us

We are Red Team, and this is our COMP3700 project at Sultan Qaboos University. Our website presents a simple system for organizing library books, members, and borrowing records.

Our objectives

  • Help readers find information about books.
  • Provide clear forms for common library tasks.
  • Organize sample records using tables.
